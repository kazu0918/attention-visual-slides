13 BUILDING Q, K, AND V

One Input,
Three Views

From the input matrix H to Q, K, and V

Self-attention starts with one matrix H: one row for every token in the sentence.

It creates three working views. Q asks what each token is looking for, K describes what each token offers, and V carries the information to collect.

For now, picture Q, K, and V as three copies of H. This conceptual shortcut makes the attention calculation easier to follow.

17 SCALING BEFORE SOFTMAX

Large Scores Make
Softmax Too Certain

Why attention divides by √dk before normalization

Softmax raises e to each score. When every score becomes larger, exponentiation magnifies even small gaps.

The ranking stays exactly the same, but the resulting attention can collapse onto one token.

As the key dimension dk grows, a dot product adds more terms and its typical magnitude grows. Dividing by √dk keeps the score scale controlled before softmax.

WHY DIVIDE BY √dk?

√dk is a scale stabilizer.

A dot product adds one product for every dimension. With more dimensions, its typical magnitude grows—even when the underlying relationship has not become stronger.

larger dk→larger QKᵀ→sharper softmax
Dividing by √dk removes this dimension-driven inflation while preserving the score order.

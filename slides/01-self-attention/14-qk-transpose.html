Matrix multiplication is a sequence of vector dot products

First, transpose K. Its token vectors change from rows into columns.

Then each row of Q meets each column of Kᵀ. Their dot product becomes one cell in the score matrix.

The row word tells us who is looking. The column word tells us which word is being compared. Here, vectors are unit-normalized, so a word compared with itself scores 1.00.
One row × one column → one attention score.

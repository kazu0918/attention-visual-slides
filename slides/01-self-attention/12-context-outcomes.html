A Score for
Vector Similarity

Similar Direction → Larger Dot Product

Words with related meanings tend to point in similar directions in an embedding space. Their dot product is therefore large.

When two word vectors point in very different directions, their dot product becomes small—or even negative.

For this intuition, the vectors are normalized to the same length. Dot product measures directional alignment, not literal distance between the arrow tips.

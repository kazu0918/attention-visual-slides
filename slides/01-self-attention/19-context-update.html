19 LAST STEP · WEIGHTS × V

Use the Weights
to Mix V

Attention finishes by combining the original token information

V comes from the original embeddings: each token embedding is passed through the learned V projection, so every row still carries that token’s information.

The attention-weight matrix now tells us how much of every V row to collect. Each output row becomes a weighted mixture of all five token vectors.

For one output token: new vector = Σ attention weight × V row. The weights do not replace the embeddings—they control how the embedding information is mixed.
Each row of attention weights mixes all rows of V.

21 MULTI-HEAD ATTENTION

Eight Heads,
Eight Questions

MultiHead(Q,K,V) = Concat(head₁, …, head₈)WO

Everything up to here was one attention head. The paper runs eight, side by side, on the same sentence.

Each head gets its own WQ, WK, WV, projecting 512 dimensions down to 64. Same input, eight different questions asked of it — then the answers are concatenated and mixed back together by one final matrix.

The paper's reason, §3.2.2: multi-head attention lets the model "jointly attend to information from different representation subspaces at different positions. With a single attention head, averaging inhibits this." One head has to blend everything into a single weighted mean, and a mean washes out distinctions.

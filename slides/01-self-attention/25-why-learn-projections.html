25 MULTI-HEAD ATTENTION · WHY W?

Do Not Just Copy H.
Learn What to Look For.

W makes the attention criterion trainable

If Q, K, and V are all copied directly from H, attention can only compare tokens using the representation already stored in H.

Learned projections let each head discover which relationships should receive high attention and which information should be carried forward.

The relationships shown here are illustrative. Heads learn useful patterns from data; we do not assign each head a fixed linguistic role.

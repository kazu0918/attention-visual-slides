07 POSITIONAL ENCODING

How the model understands position

There's no mechanism inside the encoder or decoder that accounts for the position of a word. [you eat salad] and [salad eat you] look the same to the model.

We need to somehow represent word positions so the model learns. Just a meaningful wobble is enough.

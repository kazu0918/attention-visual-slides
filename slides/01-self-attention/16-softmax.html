16 SOFTMAX

Turn Similarity
into a Distribution

Normalize each row into comparable attention weights

Each row belongs to one Query word. Its values say how similar that word is to every Key word.

Softmax normalizes across that row. Every score becomes positive, and the row adds up to exactly 1.

Why 1? It lets us read the result as a share of attention—“how should this word distribute 100% of its attention?” It also keeps the weighted Value mixture on a stable scale.

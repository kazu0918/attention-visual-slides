Attention

"Apple" can mean a fruit or a company. A word's meaning isn't fixed by itself — it's shaped by the words around it.

Attention updates a word's vector using the vectors of the surrounding context words. Here, that's shown as Apple's point shifting toward the fruit or tech cluster depending on the sentence.

Simplification: a real Transformer doesn't "look up a map" like this — it blends the actual words in that one sentence. But real contextual embeddings do show the same effect: the same word lands in noticeably different places depending on meaning.

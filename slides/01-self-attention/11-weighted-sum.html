11 THE GOAL OF ATTENTION

Update a Vector
Using Its Context

From Initial Embedding to Context-Aware Vector

After embedding, apple starts with the same initial vector wherever it appears.

Attention uses information from the other words in the sentence to update that vector. The result should represent apple as a fruit in one sentence and Apple as a company in the other.

These are simplified 2D vectors for visualization. A real Transformer updates hundreds of dimensions at once.
What should attention do? Transform one initial vector differently depending on the surrounding words.

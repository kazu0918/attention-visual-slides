02 WORDS AS NUMBERS

Turning Meaning
Into Numbers

word2vec

The core idea behind word2vec is to represent a word's meaning as a list of numbers — a vector. More numbers (dimensions) can capture finer shades of meaning.

Words with similar meanings end up close together in this numeric space.

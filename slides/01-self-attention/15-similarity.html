A Map of
Word-to-Word Similarity

Every cell answers: “How closely are these two token vectors aligned?”

The result is not yet the final attention output. It is a table of pairwise scores between every query word and every key word.

A large score means the two vectors point in similar directions. In our examples, apple ↔ juicy and Apple ↔ laptop receive strong scores.

Use the tabs on the right to move from the score matrices to the same idea drawn in 2D.

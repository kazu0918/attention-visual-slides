04 THE ACTUAL CLAIM

Attention
Already Existed

2014 fixed the reach. 2017 removed the recurrence.

This is the thing most summaries of the paper get wrong. Attention was not invented here — Bahdanau, Cho and Bengio introduced it for translation in 2014, and by 2017 it was standard equipment.

§2, in the paper's own words: attention mechanisms had become integral to sequence modelling, but "in all but a few cases… such attention mechanisms are used in conjunction with a recurrent network."

So read the title as a claim about sufficiency, not novelty. Not "attention exists" — "attention is all you need", meaning you can throw the RNN away entirely. That is a strong, falsifiable claim, and it is the one we should be judging tonight.

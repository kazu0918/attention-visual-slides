Why RNNs could not use the hardware

ht needs ht−1. That single dependency means a forty-token sentence takes forty steps that must happen in order - however many GPU cores are sitting idle.

Self-attention has no such chain. Every position is computed from the same input, so all of them can be done at once. That is the parallelism the paper is selling.

This is the paper's opening complaint, in its own words: "This inherently sequential nature precludes parallelization within training examples, which becomes critical at longer sequence lengths, as memory constraints limit batching across examples." (§1)
